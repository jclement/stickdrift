HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. One of them is "play".

1

Open a game on the big screen

A smart TV's browser, a laptop plugged into the TV, a projector. Anything that shows a web page. Turn the sound up: the games talk.

2

Everyone scans the code

Point the phone camera at the QR code, or type the five letters on stickdrift.net. Each phone gets a colour, a name and the right controller. The slowest part is waiting for Dave to find his camera app.

3

Mash buttons

On the same Wi-Fi, presses go straight from the phone to the game in about a millisecond. On guest or hotel Wi-Fi it still works, just a touch slower.

QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK

Yes, but…

"Do they have to install something?"

No. The controller is a web page. If they can open a link, they can play. If they can't open a link, you have bigger problems than this game night.

"What does it cost?"

Nothing. No ads, no accounts, no "premium bombs".

"It won't go full screen on my iPhone."

Apple doesn't let web pages do that. Add to Home Screen does, and launched from there it offers to rejoin your last game in one tap.

"Someone's phone locked mid-round."

Everything they were holding lets go, and when they unlock it they're back in the same seat. No ghost player running into a wall forever.
